# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected Behaviour

- Communicate with care, precision, and respect.
- Welcome constructive scientific and technical disagreement.
- Focus criticism on evidence, methods, code, and outcomes rather than people.
- Respect privacy, source licences, research participants, and security boundaries.
- Accept responsibility, correct mistakes, and help maintain a productive environment.

Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `info@psilocybin-research.com`. The maintainer will review reports confidentially, avoid conflicts of interest where possible, and may edit or remove contributions, issue a warning, restrict participation, or permanently exclude a participant when necessary.

This policy applies in repository discussions, project communication, and public spaces where someone is representing the project.
